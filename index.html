How We Work

From Bottle to Flake —
A Clean 6-Step Process

Every bottle that comes in is transformed through a rigorous, quality-controlled process before leaving as premium recycled PET flakes.

1
Collection
PET bottles sourced from local dealers and certified collection centers across the region.
2
Label Removal
Machine processing strips all labels and contaminants from each bottle efficiently.
3
Sorting & Cleaning
Bottles carefully sorted and cleaned to ensure only quality material proceeds further.
4
Crushing Process
Advanced crushing machines convert cleaned bottles into uniform PET flakes (chips).
5
Packing
PET flakes packed securely in 15 KG bags, ready for storage and safe transportation.
6
Supply
Finished PET flakes delivered reliably to recycling units and packaging industries.
